Introduction

The project was originally inspired by cellular automata (CA), pioneered by John von Neumann and Stanisław Ulam.
I was particularly interested in emergent systems and algorithms. The systems I researched included Particle Lenia by Alexander Mordvintsev, Lenia by Bert Chan, and Ant Colony Optimisation (ACO) by Marco Dorigo.
